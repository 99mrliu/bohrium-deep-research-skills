[a-p1]
SYNTHETIC PAPER A — Dataset A evaluates batch adsorption removal of the dye methylene blue using magnetite-biochar composite versus pristine biochar. The metric is equilibrium adsorption capacity in mg/g. The ambient applicability domain is pH 7, initial concentration 100 mg/L, 298 K, 120 min, and adsorbent dose 1 g/L. The research budget is not reported.

[a-p2]
Under the stated ambient conditions, the observed equilibrium capacity was 120 mg/g for magnetite-biochar and 80 mg/g for pristine biochar. The authors report no statistical significance test in this synthetic record. This is one laboratory batch and is not a universal claim across dyes or preparation methods.

[a-p3]
The authors speculate that the higher capacity occurs because magnetite increases micropore area. Pore area, pore blocking, surface charge, and bonding were not measured, so this causal explanation is not tested. The observation itself is limited to Dataset A and the stated conditions.
