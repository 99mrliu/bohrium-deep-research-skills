SYNTHETIC PAPER B — Dataset B evaluates batch adsorption removal of the dye methylene blue using magnetite-biochar composite versus pristine biochar. The common metric is equilibrium adsorption capacity in mg/g, and the research budget is not reported. Two scopes are reported independently: an acidic extension and an attempted ambient replication.

[b-p2]
In the acidic extension at pH 3, with initial concentration 100 mg/L, 298 K, 120 min, and adsorbent dose 1 g/L, capacities were 52 mg/g for magnetite-biochar and 50 mg/g for pristine biochar. No equivalence test or uncertainty interval is reported, so no no-difference direction is assigned. This result extends coverage to acidic conditions rather than adjudicating the ambient result.

[b-p3]
In an attempted replication of Paper A at pH 7, initial concentration 100 mg/L, 298 K, 120 min, and dose 1 g/L, Dataset B instead observed 65 mg/g for magnetite-biochar and 90 mg/g for pristine biochar. The observed direction is opposite to Paper A under the reported matched conditions. Statistical significance is not reported.

[b-p4]
Paper B used an independently synthesized batch and did not test batch chemistry as a moderator. Batch preparation, unmeasured surface chemistry, and pH-history differences remain possible explanations; none is established. The acidic and ambient datasets are not pooled.
