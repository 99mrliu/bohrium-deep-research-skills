[p1] Synthetic Paper A reports a photocatalytic textile-wastewater experiment; it is fixture text, not a real publication.
[p2] In a batch test at room temperature, synthetic textile wastewater containing Reactive Red 195 received UV/H2O2 treatment; after a 90 min observation horizon with a 90 min exposure budget, the decolorization efficiency, defined as the percentage reduction in baseline-corrected UV-vis absorbance at the dye peak, was 92%.
[p3] The authors reported no inferential pooling and treated a decrease in absorbance as the qualitative direction.
