[p1] Synthetic Paper B reports an adsorption experiment on a different dye; it is fixture text, not a real publication.
[p2] In a batch test at room temperature, synthetic aqueous dye solution containing Methylene Blue contacted activated carbon; at a fixed 90 min observation horizon, the adsorption removal fraction, defined as the percentage reduction in measured aqueous Methylene Blue concentration from initial concentration, was 92%.
[p3] The adsorbent-mass budget was not reported; a decrease in aqueous dye concentration was the qualitative direction.
