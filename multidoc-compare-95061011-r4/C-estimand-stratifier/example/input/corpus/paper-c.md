[p1] Synthetic Paper C independently repeats the same normalized estimand as Paper A with hours rather than minutes; it is fixture text, not a real publication.
[p2] In a batch test at room temperature, synthetic textile wastewater containing Reactive Red 195 received UV/H2O2 treatment; after a 1.5 h observation horizon with a 1.5 h exposure budget, the decolorization efficiency, defined as the percentage reduction in baseline-corrected UV-vis absorbance at the dye peak, was 92%.
[p3] The time and exposure-budget conversions use 1 h = 60 min exactly, and a decrease in absorbance was the qualitative direction.
