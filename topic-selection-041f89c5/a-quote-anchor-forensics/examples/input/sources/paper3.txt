Title: A Dataset for Novelty Claim Annotation in Grant Proposals
Authors: E. Fictional, F. Synthetic
Year: 2025

Abstract
We release a small expert-annotated dataset of 412 novelty claims from
grant proposals, each labeled as method, data, scenario-transfer, or
conclusion-revision novelty, with links to covering prior work. The
dataset is intended for evaluating claim-level novelty assessment
systems. Inter-annotator agreement was 0.71 (Cohen's kappa).
