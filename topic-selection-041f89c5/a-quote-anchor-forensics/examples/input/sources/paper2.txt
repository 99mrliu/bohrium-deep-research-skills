Title: Active Learning Cuts Screening Cost, Sometimes
Authors: C. Demo, D. Placeholder
Year: 2023

Abstract
Active learning is widely reported to reduce the labeling cost of
citation screening. In our reproduction across three datasets, the
benefit was substantial on balanced corpora but vanished on a highly
imbalanced corpus, where the active strategy required more labels than
random sampling. We caution against reporting only supportive results.
