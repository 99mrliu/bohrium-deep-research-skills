Title: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Screening for Systematic Review: A Controlled Study
Authors: A. Example, B. Sample
Year: 2024

Abstract
We study whether a graph-based retrieval stage improves title/abstract
screening for systematic reviews. On two public benchmark datasets, the
proposed pipeline achieved a Work Saved over Sampling of 0.61 at 95%
recall, compared with 0.44 for a keyword-only baseline. The evaluation
covers only computer science and medical review topics.

Section 2.1 Setup
All experiments were run on a single consumer GPU with 16 GB memory.
Screening decisions were audited by two independent annotators.
