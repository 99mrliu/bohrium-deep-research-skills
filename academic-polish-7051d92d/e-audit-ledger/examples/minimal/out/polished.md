Cell cultures were maintained at 37 °C in a humidified incubator with 5% CO2 [12]. The medium was replaced every 48 h and cells were passaged at 80% confluence. The absorbance was measured at a wavelength of 450 nm using a microplate reader. Using ImageJ software, we analyzed the images to quantify colony formation. We performed a series of assays to determine whether the treatment was effective. These results suggest that the new protocol is effective for the cell types tested. Because the signal was weak, the exposure time was increased to 30 s. The samples were heated to 95 °C for 10 min, then cooled on ice. Statistical significance was set at p < 0.05, and representative images are shown in Fig. 2.
