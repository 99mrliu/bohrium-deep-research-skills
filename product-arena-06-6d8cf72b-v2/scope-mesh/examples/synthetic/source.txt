SYNTHETIC / 合成示例 — The Aster-17 alloy, study, values, and source are entirely fictional.
[S1]
Methods — Thirty-two cast Aster-17 magnesium-alloy coupons from one fictional batch were allocated to plasma-electrolytic oxidation (PEO; 18 V for 12 min) or no coating, with 16 coupons per arm. Thick coupons were 2.0 mm and thin coupons were 0.8 mm; each arm contained eight coupons of each thickness. Coupons underwent a 48 h salt-spray exposure at 35 °C with 5% NaCl. Mass loss was measured in g/m^2 after exactly 48 h.
[S2]
Abstract — In this fictional laboratory study, PEO reduced pooled mean mass loss over 48 h relative to no coating in cast Aster-17 coupons. The abstract makes no claim about other alloys, field service, or comparative rankings.
[S3]
Results — Across all coupons, mean mass loss was 6.4 g/m^2 in the PEO arm and approximately 9.1 g/m^2 in the uncoated arm. The thick stratum had mean mass loss of 4.1 g/m^2 with PEO and 10.2 g/m^2 without coating. In the thin stratum, mean mass loss was 8.7 g/m^2 with PEO and 7.9 g/m^2 without coating, reversing the pooled direction.
[S4]
Table 1 — Mass loss (g/m^2), fictional means: pooled PEO 6.4, uncoated approximately 9.1; thick PEO 4.1, uncoated 10.2; thin PEO 8.7, uncoated 7.9. Each cell summarizes eight coupons from the same single batch; no confidence intervals were reported.
[S5]
Discussion — The authors state that PEO appears promising for the tested thick Aster-17 coupons, but caution that the thin stratum reversed the direction. The authors speculate that a higher edge-to-face ratio may contribute to the thin-coupon reversal, but no mechanism was established. The study was not designed to test service life, other alloys, field environments, or rankings against other coating methods.
