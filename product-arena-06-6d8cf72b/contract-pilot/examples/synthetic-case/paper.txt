Bamboo-fiber composite panels for interior acoustics
T. Mock and U. Sample, Lab of Invented Materials

Abstract
We pressed bamboo-fiber composite panels of 12 mm thickness and measured
their sound absorption and bending strength. The panels reached a mean
bending strength of 48 MPa and an absorption coefficient of 0.62 at
1 kHz in impedance-tube tests.

1. Methods
Fibers were mixed with a bio-based binder, hot-pressed into 12 mm
panels, and conditioned at 20 C and 50 percent humidity for one week.
Bending strength followed a three-point setup; absorption used an
impedance tube.

2. Results
Mean bending strength was 48 MPa (n = 5, sd = 4). The absorption
coefficient at 1 kHz was 0.62. Density averaged 0.71 g/cm3. No
long-term humidity cycling was performed.

3. Conclusion
The panels combine moderate strength with useful absorption at 1 kHz;
durability under humidity cycling remains an open question.
