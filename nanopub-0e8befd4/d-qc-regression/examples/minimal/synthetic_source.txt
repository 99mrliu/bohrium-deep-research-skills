PaperTrail: a citation integrity checker for open-access corpora
L. Invented, M. Notreal. Synthetic Software Studies, 2024. (fictional)

Abstract
PaperTrail is a citation integrity checker that scans open-access corpora for
broken identifier links. On a crawl of 12,000 articles, PaperTrail flagged 3.2%
of DOI links as unresolvable and completed the scan in 4.5 hours. The tool
supports 5 identifier schemes, including DOI and arXiv. We suggest that the
same pipeline may extend to preprint repositories. The current release checks
only identifier-based links, not URL-only references.
