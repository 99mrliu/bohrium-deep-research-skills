Hierarchical Porous Nitrogen-Doped Carbon NPC-800 for Aqueous Zinc-Ion Batteries
（分级多孔氮掺杂碳 NPC-800 用于水系锌离子电池）

L. Chen, M. Okafor, R. Tanabe
Institute of Synthetic Energy Materials, Westbridge University（虚构机构）

Abstract
We report a nitrogen-doped porous carbon, denoted NPC-800, synthesized by
one-step pyrolysis of a zeolitic imidazolate framework precursor at 800 °C.
When evaluated as the cathode host for aqueous zinc-ion batteries, NPC-800
delivers a specific capacity of 412 mAh/g after 200 cycles at 0.1 A/g,
corresponding to a capacity retention of 91%. The hierarchical pore structure
facilitates electrolyte infiltration and shortens ion diffusion pathways.
我们报道了一种氮掺杂多孔碳 NPC-800。作为水系锌离子电池正极宿主，NPC-800
在 0.1 A/g 下循环 200 次后比容量为 412 mAh/g，容量保持率 91%。分级孔结构
有利于电解液浸润并缩短离子扩散路径。

Keywords: zinc-ion battery; nitrogen doping; porous carbon; capacity retention

1. Introduction（节选）
Aqueous zinc-ion batteries are attractive for grid-scale storage owing to their
safety and low cost. However, cathode hosts often suffer from sluggish ion
transport. Hierarchical porosity has been widely discussed as a structural
strategy to mitigate this limitation, although direct causal attribution in
specific materials remains difficult to establish.

2. Experimental Section（节选）
NPC-800 was prepared by pyrolyzing ZIF-8 precursor at 800 °C under argon for
3 h. For comparison, NPC-900 was obtained at 900 °C. Nitrogen content measured
by XPS was 8.7 at% for NPC-800 and 4.2 at% for NPC-900. Electrochemical tests
were conducted in 2 M ZnSO4 electrolyte at ambient temperature (25 °C).
所有电化学测试均在 25 °C 环境温度下进行；未进行高温或低温工况测试。

3. Results and Discussion（节选）

3.1 Rate performance. NPC-800 delivers reversible capacities of 412, 356,
301, and 265 mAh/g at current densities of 0.1, 0.5, 1, and 5 A/g,
respectively. At 10 A/g the capacity drops to 178 mAh/g.

3.2 Long-term cycling. At 1 A/g, NPC-800 retains 78% of its initial capacity
after 1000 cycles (Figure 4). The control sample NPC-900 retains 71% under
identical conditions.

3.3 Comparison with literature. The initial coulombic efficiency of NPC-800
is 86%, lower than the 92% reported for a commercial activated carbon
reference under comparable conditions.

3.4 Mechanism discussion. We tentatively attribute the capacity advantage of
NPC-800 over NPC-900 in part to its higher nitrogen content, which may
introduce additional redox-active sites. We note, however, that the
contribution of defect sites and residual zinc adsorption cannot be excluded,
and the present data do not allow a definitive causal assignment.
我们推测 NPC-800 的容量优势部分来源于更高的氮含量，其可能引入额外的氧化
还原活性位点；但缺陷位点与残余锌吸附的贡献不可排除，现有数据不足以给出
确定性的因果归因。分级孔结构在多个样品中均与更好的倍率表现相关（相关性
观察，非因果证明）。

4. Conclusion
NPC-800 shows promising capacity and cycling stability as a cathode host for
aqueous zinc-ion batteries. Future work will quantify the respective
contributions of nitrogen dopants and defect sites.
