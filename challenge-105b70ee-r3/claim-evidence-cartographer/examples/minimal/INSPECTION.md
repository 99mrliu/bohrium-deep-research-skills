## Direct content inspection

I opened the generated result, graph, and Markdown map after execution. The following were observed in the files, not merely asserted by the documentation:

1. `catalyst_A_yield` has scalar 82 and `% yield`, mean of three independent runs, SD = 2 percentage points, primary P2 quote, comparator B = 61% with SD = 3 percentage points, and an additional P1 condition quotation for 20 mg catalyst, 1.0 mmol substrate, 5 mL water, 80 °C, 6 h, 1 atm N2, and calibrated GC/internal standard. Both condition aliases are identical.
2. The author conclusion is a distinct `CL-CONC` node grounded in P3 and linked to both `OBS-A` and `OBS-B`. It retains “under the tested conditions.” The graph does not represent its support edge as proof.
3. `desorption_mechanism` is explicitly labeled “Untested author hypothesis,” retains `speculate`, `may`, and `not tested` in `CL-HYP`, has `promotion_allowed: false`, and uses P5 as primary evidence. It was not promoted to an established conclusion.
4. `stability_beyond_6h` is `not_reported` with null scalar, units, and statistic, plus exact P4 evidence “did not measure stability beyond 6 h.” This is a completed scoped non-report; together with the other adjudicated fields it yields overall `answered`.
5. The 90% cited value is attributed to Chen et al. in a `prior_work_cited_claim`, not a current result. `could`, `suggesting`, and `might` survive. Its only condition supported by this excerpt is 100 °C for a related catalyst; no full bibliographic record or like-for-like comparison was invented.
6. The method observation and all narrative quotes are visible in the readable Markdown map, not hidden only in graph internals.
