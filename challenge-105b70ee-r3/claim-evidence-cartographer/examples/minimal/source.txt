[P1 — Methods]
We prepared catalyst A and comparator catalyst B for this synthetic study. All reactions used 20 mg catalyst, 1.0 mmol substrate, 5 mL water, 80 °C for 6 h under 1 atm N2. Product yield was measured by calibrated GC using an internal standard.

[P2 — Results]
Under these conditions, catalyst A gave 82% yield, whereas catalyst B gave 61% yield in a paired run; each value is the mean of three independent runs, and the standard deviations were 2 and 3 percentage points, respectively.

[P3 — Discussion]
These observations lead us to conclude that catalyst A is more active than catalyst B under the tested conditions.

[P4 — Limitations]
We tested only one substrate and did not measure stability beyond 6 h; therefore, broader substrate generality and long-term durability remain unknown.

[P5 — Hypothesis]
We speculate that the higher yield may arise from faster intermediate desorption, but this mechanism was not tested.

[P6 — Prior work]
Chen et al. reported that a related catalyst could reach 90% yield at 100 °C, suggesting that higher temperature might improve performance.
