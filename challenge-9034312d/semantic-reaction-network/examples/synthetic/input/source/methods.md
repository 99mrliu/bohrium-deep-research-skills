# SYNTHETIC assay methods

This is a pedagogical fixture, not a report of experiments. A hypothetical synthetic substrate S is incubated with hypothetical synthetic enzyme E. In one condition hypothetical synthetic inhibitor I is also present and reduces product formation. No molecular inhibition class is specified. Product formation is observed by a hypothetical absorbance readout after the reaction stage.

The source table `data/assay.csv` contains actual text rows parsed by the script, but all numbers are invented for this synthetic demonstration. `time_min` is plotted on the x-axis, `product_uM` on the y-axis, and `condition` defines the two series. `replicate_label` is deliberately not plotted. No real biological source or measurement is implied.
