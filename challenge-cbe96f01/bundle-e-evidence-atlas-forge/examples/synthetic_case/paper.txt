A Synthetic Study of Nap Interventions

We recruited 120 participants and assigned them to nap and control
groups. The nap group improved recall by 18.2 percent relative to
baseline, and the difference was significant (p = 0.049). Prior work has
shown that sleep supports declarative memory [1]. Data and code are
available on request.
