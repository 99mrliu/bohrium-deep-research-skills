Abstract

We recruited 120 participants for a nap intervention study. The nap
intervention improved recall by 52.5% (55/120) relative to baseline, a
highly significant effect (p = 0.049).

Methods

A total of 118 participants were enrolled after screening. The nap group
contained 60 participants and the control group contained 62
participants. Baseline recall score was 12.3 ± 2.1 points.

Results

Improvement counts by group:

| Group | Improved | Not improved | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nap | 30 | 28 | 58 |
| Control | 25 | 37 | 62 |
| Total | 55 | 65 | 999 |

The between-group difference in improvement rates was significant
(p = 0.083). Mean improvement was 4.6 points (95% CI, 1.2 to 8.0).
