# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — 合成任务书（非真实案例）

Prepare a conference-review pitch for the project **SOLV-GNN** (a graph neural
network that screens battery electrolyte solvents; all facts here are
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE fabrications for demonstration only).

Constraints (the work order):

- Audience: a mixed review committee (评审委员会) — a few battery specialists,
  several ML generalists, one program officer.
- Duration: 12 minutes of talking, no more.
- Length: exactly 10 slides in the main storyline; backup slides are allowed
  but do not count toward the 10.
- Venue: review (funding panel), so the conclusion must come early.
- Language: English.

Goal: Convince the review committee that the proposed solvent-screening
method is novel and that the preliminary evidence is strong enough to fund.

The storyboard must give every slide exactly one message, a narrative role,
speaker notes sized to the time budget, and exhibit locators for every
result or comparison slide.
