Definition and formula
----------------------
For a x^2 + b x + c = 0 with a != 0 and discriminant b^2 - 4ac >= 0, the two real roots satisfy:

x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / (2a).

Vieta identities are x1 + x2 = -b/a and x1*x2 = c/a. Multiplying all three coefficients by the same nonzero scale leaves roots invariant.
