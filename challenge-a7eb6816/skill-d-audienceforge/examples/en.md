# Engineering Phaeodactylum tricornutum for Enhanced Carbon Fixation (English Version)

Inspired by Carbon Capture, Utilization and Storage (CCUS), we rewired the cellular metabolism of Phaeodactylum tricornutum, raising the biological carbon fixation rate by 42%.

This cell-level CCUS strategy also promoted fucoxanthin accumulation. The engineered Phaeodactylum tricornutum strain remained stable in a one-litre photobioreactor.
