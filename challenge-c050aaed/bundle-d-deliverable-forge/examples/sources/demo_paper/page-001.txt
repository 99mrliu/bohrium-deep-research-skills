Demo Paper: A Fictional Study of Spaced Retrieval in Vocabulary Learning
Page 1

We study whether spaced retrieval practice improves long-term vocabulary
retention compared with massed rehearsal. Our synthetic cohort of 120 adult
learners was randomly assigned to two synthetic conditions.

The spaced group reviewed each target word at expanding intervals over
four weeks, while the massed group completed the same total exposure in a
single week. All participants completed an identical immediate post-test.
