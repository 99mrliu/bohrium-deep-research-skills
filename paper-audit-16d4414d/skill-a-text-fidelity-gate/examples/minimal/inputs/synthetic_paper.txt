Effects of Compound X on Seedling Growth: A Controlled Pot Trial

Abstract
We tested whether Compound X changes seedling height. Forty pots were
randomized to control or treatment. Treated seedlings were taller on
average. We discuss implications for greenhouse practice.

1. Introduction
Compound X is widely used in horticulture. Whether it improves early
growth remains contested. This study asks: does Compound X increase
seedling height under controlled conditions?

2. Methods
Forty pots were randomized, twenty per arm. Height was measured at day 21
by a blinded rater. The significance threshold was p < 0.05.

3. Results
Mean height was 12.4 cm in the control arm and 15.1 cm in the treatment
arm. The difference was statistically significant (p = 0.03).
