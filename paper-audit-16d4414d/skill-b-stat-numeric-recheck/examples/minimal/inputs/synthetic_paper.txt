Compound Y and Root Elongation: A Short Report

Abstract
We grew seedlings with Compound Y. Treated roots averaged 15.1 cm. The
gain was statistically significant. Ninety percent of seeds germinated.

1. Introduction
Root elongation drives early vigor. The role of Compound Y is unclear.

2. Methods
Twenty pots per arm were randomized. The significance threshold was
p < 0.05. Height was measured at day 21.

3. Results
Mean root length in controls was 12.4 cm (95% CI 13.9, 16.9). Treated
roots averaged 14.1 cm, not 15.1 cm as previewed. The treatment arm
showed higher survival than control (82 vs 91%). The difference was
statistically significant (p = 0.31). 90% (17/20) of seeds germinated in
the treatment arm. The dose response was significant (p = 1.20).

4. Discussion
Compound Y may aid rooting, but our numbers speak softly.
