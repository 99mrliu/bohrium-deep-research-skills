Abstract
========
This synthetic demo paper INTENTIONALLY states the year 2022 in its text
while the EuropePMC snapshot metadata claims an earlier publication year.
The a-source-verifier crosscheck step is expected to flag this as a YEAR
MISMATCH (e.g., a preprint-vs-published version conflict or a stale
snapshot). Everything here is fictional and exists only to test the
verification pipeline.

1. Introduction
===============
Imaginary metrics were computed over hypothetical datasets. The text
deliberately keeps the title, DOI and first-author surname consistent with
the snapshot so that ONLY the year check fails, demonstrating per-field
mismatch reporting.

2. Note
=======
The snapshot's year disagrees with this document's stated year of 2022.
The pipeline must report the discrepancy as an uncertainty, never silently
repair it.
