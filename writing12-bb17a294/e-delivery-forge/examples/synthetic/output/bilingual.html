合成示例 / Synthetic Example: 光子晶格中的合成维度 Synthetic Dimensions in Photonic Lattices

1 引言 Introduction

⟦S0001⟧

Synthetic dimensions formed by internal degrees of freedom offer a compact route to high-dimensional lattice physics in photonic systems.

由内部自由度构成的合成维度为在光子系统中实现高维晶格物理提供了一条紧凑的途径。

⟦S0002⟧

In this synthetic example, we consider a ring resonator whose frequency modes serve as lattice sites along a synthetic frequency axis.

在这一合成示例中，我们考虑一个环形谐振腔，其频率模式充当沿合成频率轴排列的格点。

2 结论 Conclusion

⟦S0006⟧

Our synthetic study suggests that frequency synthetic dimensions can emulate two-dimensional topological lattice models in a single resonator.

我们的合成研究表明，频率合成维度能够在单个谐振腔中模拟二维拓扑晶格模型。
