# Synthetic Example / 合成示例: A Note on Perovskite Photovoltaics

Perovskite solar cell devices have reached a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) above 25%. The PCE of a perovskite solar cell depends strongly on charge carrier mobility and grain boundary density. A higher charge carrier mobility reduces recombination losses in the absorber layer.

## Fabrication by Spin Coating

Spin coating remains the standard lab-scale route for the perovskite solar cell. During spin coating, the precursor solution wets the substrate and forms a uniform film. Grain boundary passivation during spin coating improves the open-circuit voltage of the cell. The open-circuit voltage rises when grain boundary defects are suppressed and charge carrier mobility is preserved.

## Device Metrics
