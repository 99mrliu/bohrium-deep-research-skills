Perovskite Solar Cells under Mechanical Bending

Abstract

Perovskite photovoltaic devices are promising for flexible power sources, yet their electri-
cal performance under repeated bending remains poorly understood. We report bending-fatigue
measurements on 120 devices across three curvature radii.

Introduction

The power conversion efficiency of perovskite solar cells has risen above 26% in laboratory
cells. For flexible applications, however, the active layer must survive thousands of bending
cycles without cracking. Cracks nucleate at grain boundaries and propagate across the absorber,
which raises series resistance and reduces fill factor.
