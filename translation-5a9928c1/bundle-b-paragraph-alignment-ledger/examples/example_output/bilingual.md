Perovskite Solar Cells under Mechanical Bending

机械弯曲下的钙钛矿太阳能电池

## p002 [heading, page 1]
Abstract

摘要

## p003 [paragraph, page 1]
Perovskite photovoltaic devices are promising for flexible power sources, yet their electrical performance under repeated bending remains poorly understood. We report bending-fatigue measurements on 120 devices across three curvature radii.

钙钛矿光伏器件是柔性电源的有希望候选，但其在反复弯曲下的电学性能仍缺乏深入理解。我们报道了 120 个器件在三种弯曲半径下的弯曲疲劳测量。

## p004 [heading, page 1]
Introduction

引言

## p005 [paragraph, page 1]
The power conversion efficiency of perovskite solar cells has risen above 26% in laboratory cells. For flexible applications, however, the active layer must survive thousands of bending cycles without cracking. Cracks nucleate at grain boundaries and propagate across the absorber, which raises series resistance and reduces fill factor.

钙钛矿太阳能电池的实验室光电转换效率已超过 26%。然而对于柔性应用，活性层必须在不开裂的前提下经受数千次弯曲循环。裂纹在晶界处形核并穿过吸光层扩展，从而提高串联电阻并降低填充因子。

## p008 [heading, page 2]
Discussion

讨论

## p009 [paragraph, page 2]
The fatigue crack density follows a Paris-law-type relation with the bending strain amplitude. Devices with a 10 mm radius retained over 90% of initial efficiency after 10 000 cycles, whereas 3 mm-radius devices fell below 60%.

疲劳裂纹密度随弯曲应变幅呈 Paris 律型关系。半径 10 mm 的器件在 10 000 次循环后仍保持初始效率的 90% 以上，而半径 3 mm 的器件降至 60% 以下。
