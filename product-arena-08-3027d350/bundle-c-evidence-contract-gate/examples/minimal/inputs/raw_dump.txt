1. Introduction

The synthetic study investigates column ordering in scientific PDFs. Text
extraction must preserve the original reading order across both columns.
Every extracted paragraph carries a page number and a bounding box.

2. Method

Word coordinates are clustered into lines, and lines into blocks by
vertical gap analysis. The header and footer are removed by position and
cross-page repetition. All numbers in this example are invented.
