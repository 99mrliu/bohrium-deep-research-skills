2.3 Synthetic reaction screening

The synthetic example screens three catalysts under identical conditions.
All numbers are invented for demonstration only and carry no experimental
meaning. The screening results are collected in Table 1 below.

Table 1. Synthetic reaction conditions and yields (invented data).

Entry    Catalyst                 Loading (mg)        Yield (%)
  1      Pd(OAc)2                 5                   82
  2      Pd(PPh3)4                10                  91
  3      Ni(cod)2                 20                  not detected
                                                      (below 1 %)
  4      CuI                                          45

Yields were determined by synthetic integration. Entry 4 lacks a loading
value in the source layout; the empty cell is left unresolved rather than
guessed. Further spectroscopic data appear in Table 2 on the next page.
