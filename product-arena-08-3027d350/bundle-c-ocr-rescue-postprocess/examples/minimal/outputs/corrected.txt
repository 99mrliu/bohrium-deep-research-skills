The chemical composition of the rnetal alloy was checked by X-ray
diffraction and electron spectroscopy. The analysis showed a
crystalline lattice with no secondary phase. A small photon flux
from the synchrotron source was used for the measurement. The
spectroscopy data were recorded at room temperature under vacuum.
The catalyst was prepared by annealing the precursor at high
temperature. During the reaction, α-particles from the source were
counted, and the gamma background was subtracted. The final spectrum
showed one broad peak near 532 nm, together with a shoulder at
548 nm. This feature was assigned to a surface plasmon resonance
of the finished film.
Comparison with the vvavelength of the laser line confirmed the
assignment. The grain size was about 40 nm and the layer was
uniform, although one region gave a corrupted signal [unreadable] and had
to be excluded. The scan step was l mV for all runs.
